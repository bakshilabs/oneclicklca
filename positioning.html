Positioning

The homepage promise, "Do more LCAs and EPDs, faster", speaks to the practitioner who runs the tool. The people who sign want a permit, a credit, a bid or a specification, so each buyer gets its own message built on the platform story.

Proposals for discussion. Every message here would be tested in sales calls and campaigns before it replaced anything live.

    The statement as one sentence

    For design, construction and manufacturing firms that have to prove the carbon of their buildings and products, One Click LCA is the LCA platform that turns each market's rules into a finished, verifiable result in the least time. It holds the largest construction LCA database, covers more than a hundred standards in one model and automates EPDs from data to publication. Firms use it to win permits, certifications, bids and specifications in every country they work in.
