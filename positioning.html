Positioning

The homepage promise, "Do more LCAs and EPDs, faster", speaks to the practitioner who runs the tool. The people who sign want a permit, a credit, a bid or a specification, so each buyer gets its own message built on the platform story.

Proposals for discussion. Every message here would be tested in sales calls and campaigns before it replaced anything live.
