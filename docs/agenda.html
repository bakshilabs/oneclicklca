The argument

The material in the meeting makes one case in four steps. Each step is shown on a meeting page and backed by an appendix page.

  1. 1

    The market widens in 2028.

    From 1 January 2028 every new EU building over 1,000 m² must disclose its life cycle carbon. Most of the design teams this reaches have never bought an LCA tool.

    The 2028 BuyerBacked by A3 Demand Drivers
  2. 2

    The new buyer's alternative is waiting.

    A first-time buyer can hire a consultant for one project or wait for national limit values. I expect no decision to be the largest loss in these segments, and the CRM needs a loss reason to show it.

    The 2028 BuyerBacked by A4 Buyers and A6 Alternatives
  3. 3

    The business case wins the waiting buyer.

    Value for money is the lowest Capterra sub-score at 4.0 of 5, while support scores 4.9. A seller who sets a consultant's fees against a licence gives the waiting buyer a reason to act this year.

    The 2028 BuyerBacked by A9 Enablement
  4. 4

    The first launch follows from the first three.

    I would run a tier one launch in 2027 for first-time buyers in Germany, Italy, Spain and global engineering firms, give sellers a business case kit, and judge it on win rate and the no-decision share in those segments.

    Launch ProposalBacked by A5 Segment Model and A8 Launch Playbook
