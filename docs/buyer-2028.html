The 2028 Buyer

From 1 January 2028 every new building over 1,000 m² in the EU must disclose its life cycle carbon. Three findings describe the design team that this rule turns into a buyer, and each one changes what product marketing should do in 2027.

Each finding links to the appendix page that holds the sources and the full model.

Finding one

Twenty countries get their first rule at once

Seven EU countries require a life cycle carbon calculation today. The other twenty, including Germany, Italy and Spain, will have no national rule until the EU disclosure date arrives for all of them on the same day.

So what. Most of the new demand in 2028 comes from design teams that have never chosen an LCA tool. They need to learn what an LCA is before they compare vendors.

Finding two

The first-time buyer's alternative is to wait

A design team facing its first disclosure can hire a consultant for each project, use a free national tool or wait until a client asks. Each of these delays a licence decision, so the deal is most often lost to no decision.

So what. Battlecards against named rivals help less in this segment than a reason to start before the deadline. The CRM needs a "no decision" loss reason before any of this can be measured.

Finding three

Value for money is the weakest score

On Capterra, value for money scores 4.0 of 5 while support scores 4.9. Buyers who stay find the product worth it, and buyers who are new to LCA cannot yet see why it costs what it does.

So what. The business case is the asset that wins a waiting buyer. At the default figures a design firm breaks even at five assessments a year, which a seller can show on a call.

What the findings ask of product marketing

Three consequences for 2027

  1. Teach before sellingThe first message explains the EU method and the first project, because most of these buyers have never run an LCA. Webinars with green building councils carry this better than a feature page.See the Launch Proposal
  2. Arm sellers against waitingThe kit leads with the cost of doing nothing and the business case calculator. Battlecards against named competitors come second for this segment.A9 Enablement
  3. Measure the no-decision shareTwo new CRM fields, alternative and loss reason, let the company see whether the launch reduces no-decision losses in the target segments.A10 Win Rate
