EPD Generator Campaign

Core messaging for a six-month EPD Generator amplification campaign in the UK starting in Q1 2027, a landing page wireframe with the copy written in full, and the rationale. The first section records how I did the work, step by step. Everything is based on public sources and the product as it is today.

All message lines on this page are drafts to test. Placeholder marks proof that needs One Click LCA's own data or a customer's permission. Public marks proof taken from a public source listed at the end of the page.

Part 1 · Messaging framework

Target buyer and trigger moment

The campaign targets one buyer: the commercial director at a mid-sized UK construction product manufacturer with no LCA team. The managing director signs, and the sustainability or technical manager runs the tool.

Buyer

Commercial director, mid-sized UK product manufacturer

Company
Roughly 100 to 1,000 staff, one to four UK plants, often family or private equity owned. My estimate of the core of the market.
Range
Dozens to a few hundred products in a handful of families, such as bricks, blocks, drainage, insulation, façade or roofing systems.
EPDs today
A few consultant-made EPDs for flagship products, or a trade association's sector EPD. A UK government study in 2025 found a third of the products it sampled had no UK-based EPD.
Sells through
Builders' merchants, main contractors and specifications written by architects and engineers, often through NBS.
Measured on
Revenue, margin and specification share with the main contractors and specifiers.
Worry
Paying £15,000 to £27,000 per consultant EPD for documents that sit on a shelf, and losing projects to a competitor that looks lower-carbon on paper.
Trigger moment

A product is swapped out because it has no EPD

  1. A design team models a London or BREEAM scheme in a carbon tool, as the planning authority or the client requires.
  2. The tool has no EPD for this manufacturer's product, so the model uses a UK average value. Any better-than-average performance does not show.
  3. The main contractor asks suppliers for product-specific EPDs. A competitor provides one, earns the BREEAM EPD points and takes the specification.
  4. The commercial director hears about it from a merchant or the contractor after the decision.

Why Q1 2027. The draft London Plan published in July 2026 adds upfront carbon targets, with adoption expected in early 2028, so London schemes designed during 2027 are planned against them. The UK Net Zero Carbon Buildings Standard began verifying buildings in July 2026. The ICE database stopped being licensed for commercial use on 30 September 2026, so UK assessors are changing their data sources now.

Part 1 · Draft to test

Positioning statement

For commercial directors at mid-sized UK construction product manufacturers without an LCA team, One Click LCA EPD Generator is the way to get every product in the range counted at its own verified carbon figure in the design tools where specifications are decided. A sustainability or technical manager produces the EPDs in-house at a fixed cost per EPD. They count towards BREEAM, they answer contractors' supply chain requests, and they appear inside One Click LCA, where UK designers can select them. Consultant EPDs arrive one product at a time and leave the rest of the range on UK averages.

Part 1 · Draft to test

Core narrative

The story runs in five beats. Each campaign asset tells all five or starts at one of them.

  1. Carbon is calculated at design stageThe London Plan requires a whole life carbon assessment for referable schemes. BREEAM, the UK Net Zero Carbon Buildings Standard and a growing number of local plans ask for the same calculation before the specification is fixed.
  2. Missing data hides your advantageWhen a product has no EPD, the assessor uses a UK average or a sector EPD. A better-than-average product looks average, and RICS asks the assessor to add uncertainty when generic data is used.
  3. The loss is silentThe BREEAM points and the contractor's preference go to the competitor with an EPD. The decision is made before the sales team hears about the project.
  4. The range can be covered without an LCA teamA sustainability or technical manager turns plant and product data into verified EPDs at a fixed cost each, and publishes them where designers will find them.
  5. Start with what customers specify mostThe first product families to cover are the ones that appear most often in customers' projects in 2027, while those designs and London's new targets are still being set.

Part 3

Rationale

Assumptions

  • UK designers using One Click LCA select a product-specific EPD when one is available.
  • The buyer's products perform better than the UK average. A product worse than average still gains BREEAM points and contractor access, so the gap check shows both.
  • The commercial director starts the purchase when a contractor or specifier asks, and the managing director signs it.
  • One Click LCA's usage data shows generic-data use by product category. The gap check and the placeholder proof depend on this.

Validating the messaging

  • Eight to ten interviews with commercial and managing directors at UK manufacturers, testing the trigger moment and the H1 against "faster, compliant, cheaper".
  • A win and loss review of 2026 UK EPD Generator deals.
  • A LinkedIn test of two headlines to commercial roles at UK manufacturers, measured on gap-check requests.
  • Two weeks of sales calls using the narrative, with sellers logging which beat moved the conversation.

Extending to other channels

  • Sales: the gap check becomes the first-call agenda, run on each prospect's product list.
  • Associations: copy the Recolight model for lighting into other sectors, starting with ASBP and British Precast members.
  • Specifiers: an NBS and RIBA piece on how generic values hide better products, so architects ask suppliers for EPDs.
  • Contractors: supply chain sessions with tier 1 contractors that already ask for EPDs.
  • Email: a sequence for gap-check leads, one product family at a time.
