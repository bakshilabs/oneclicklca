Example

EU disclosure for new buildings

The EU will require new buildings to disclose their life cycle carbon. Each step below applies my approach to that requirement.

The trigger

An EU rule with a fixed start

The Energy Performance of Buildings Directive requires life cycle carbon to be disclosed for new buildings, starting with larger ones.

The rule applies in every EU country on the same start date, including countries with no national rule of their own.

Who has to act

Design teams new to LCA

Public sources suggest that many design teams in countries without a national rule have not run an LCA before.

  • The user is the architect or engineer who runs the assessment.
  • The signer is the practice leader who wants the permit and the client relationship.
What they do instead

Wait, outsource or use a free tool

  • Wait until a client asks for the calculation.
  • Hire a consultant for each project.
  • Use a free national tool where one exists.

Each of these delays a licence decision, so I would expect many lost deals to end with no decision.

How I would test it

Talk to buyers and sellers first

  • Conversations with design firms in my network and in the target countries.
  • Time with the sellers who cover those countries.
  • Loss reasons in the CRM, if they are recorded.

I would change the plan where these disagree with the public sources.

Questions

What I would want to learn first

  • For sellersWhich countries are already asking about the EU requirement, and who is asking?
  • For sellersWhen a deal is lost in these countries, what does the buyer do instead?
  • For buyersWho in the practice would decide to buy a tool, and what would they need to see?
  • For buyersWhat would make them start before a client asks for the calculation?
  • For buyersHave they used a consultant or a free tool, and what did they think of it?
  • For the CRMIs no decision recorded as a loss reason, separately from losses to competitors?
For product marketing

What this means for product marketing

Explain the method first

Teams new to LCA need to understand the method and their first project before they compare tools.

Show the cost of waiting

Sellers need a simple way to compare waiting or outsourcing with buying a licence, using the buyer's own figures.

Record no decision

If no decision is recorded as a loss reason, the company can see whether the work reduces it.
