What the buying side teaches

Four things I learnt from selling to these buyers

The trigger is almost always a date

Clients bought carbon work when a permit, a certification or a board target set a deadline. Without a date, the work slipped to next year.

The user and the signer want different things

The analyst wants fewer hours. The partner wants the fee, the credit or the win. A pitch that serves only one of them stalls.

Visual evidence wins the room

A chart that shows two design options against a limit does more than a report. Buyers decide when they can see the consequence.

Trust is built by the person on the call

Clients came back because someone answered fast and knew the rule. The One Click LCA reviews say the same about its support team.
