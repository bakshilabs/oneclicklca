Plan and manage launches

Launch Playbook

One Click LCA ships a major update every month and holds about two live launch events a year. Treating every release the same either exhausts sales or buries the releases that matter. The playbook starts with a rule for how much launch each release gets.

Tiering

How big a launch does this release need?

Describe the release

Answer six questions. The tier and its launch kit update as you go.

Worked example

Tier one launch: ready for the EU 2028 disclosure

A launch built around the EU life cycle GWP method, timed to the national roadmaps due on 1 January 2027 and aimed at first-time buyers before the 1 January 2028 deadline.

Goal

Turn the 2028 deadline into new logos in the large EU markets that have no national LCA habit.

Adoption target

Agreed with sales before release, for example new Business and Designer licences in Germany, Italy and Spain in the first two quarters.

Priority segments

Chosen with the Segment Model using compliance weights:

  • German designers, first-time buyers
  • Italian and Spanish designers
  • Global engineering firms standardising one EU method

Core message

"The EU method, done once, accepted everywhere you build." The proof: the EU method on the same model as national rules and certifications, an EPC-ready report, and local support.
