Launch Playbook

    Worked example

    EU 2028 launch plan

    A tier one launch built around the EU life cycle GWP method, aimed at first-time buyers in the twelve-month window between national roadmaps (1 January 2027) and EU disclosure (1 January 2028).

    Goal

    New logos in large EU markets with no national LCA habit. Example target: Business and Designer licences in Germany, Italy and Spain within two quarters.

    Priority segments

    German designers, first-time buyersItalian and Spanish designersGlobal engineering firms

    Chosen with the Segment Model.

    Core message

    "The EU method, done once, accepted everywhere you build."

    Proof: EU method on the same model as national rules, an EPC-ready report, local support.
