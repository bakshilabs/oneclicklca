Cost of doing nothing

What waiting costs each buyer

Manufacturer

Consultant EPDs cost €5,000 to €20,000 each and take months. Only 22% of manufacturers have EPDs for more than half their portfolio, while 65% of AEC professionals say they require EPDs on all or most projects.

The cost

Products missing from specifications, plus default values in designers' models that make the product look worse than it is.

Architect or engineer

Where limits apply, an assessment that fails late means redesign. Outsourcing each assessment adds consultant fees and a dependency on someone else's timetable.

The cost

Permit delay, redesign hours, and lost fees for carbon work the firm could sell itself.

Contractor

Tenders increasingly score carbon, and Denmark now limits site emissions. Supplier EPDs arrive after the bid if they arrive at all.

The cost

Lower tender scores, and margin lost when low-carbon substitutions are made after contract.

Developer or owner

From 2028, life cycle GWP sits on the energy certificate of every large new building in the EU. Investors already read Taxonomy and GRESB results.

The cost

Buildings that look worse to investors and tenants than competitors' buildings, and late, expensive changes to meet national limits from 2030.

The calculator on the Enablement page turns these into a business case a seller can fill in with the buyer.

Exposure

Six risks a product marketer should watch

  1. Value for money is the weakest review scoreCapterra rates it 4.0, the lowest sub-score. Every free alternative makes this worse unless the business case is in the seller's hands.
  2. Low-cost national tools hold the single-country buyerLCAbyg, IVL BM and the French and Dutch tools are official, local and cheaper. The counter is the second country, the certification and the 2028 EU method.
  3. "First and only RICS-validated" has expiredCarboniCa is now also validated. Claims in UK materials need updating to "first".
  4. Startups use the speed and AI messageEmidat, Pathways and Makersite sell AI-led EPDs. Speed alone no longer separates One Click LCA; verified results at scale do.
  5. Programme operators pre-verify many toolsThat makes "verified EPD in weeks" common. Differentiation moves to cost per EPD across a range, data quality and what happens after publication.
  6. Two group products overlapSimaPro and the EPD Generator both serve manufacturers. Without a clear rule, sellers compete with each other and SimaPro resellers worry.
