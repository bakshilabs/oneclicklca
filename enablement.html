Enablement

Sellers and resellers have to explain an LCA product to a buyer who is not an LCA expert, and justify its price against a free tool. Enablement works when a seller can use it on a live call without product marketing in the room. Everything on this page is built for that test.

Discovery

Questions that find the trigger and the signer

Design firms

  • Which projects this year need a whole life carbon result, and for which rule or scheme?
  • Who does those assessments today, and what does each one cost in fees or hours?
  • What happens if a result fails a limit late in design?
  • Which BIM tools does the team model in?
  • Who would sign for a firm-wide tool?

Contractors

  • How many tenders in the past year scored carbon, and how did you score?
  • When do you get supplier EPDs, before or after the bid?
  • Which clients ask for as-built carbon reporting?
  • Do site emission limits or CSRD apply to you?
  • Who owns the bid budget?

Manufacturers

  • How many products do you sell, and how many have EPDs today?
  • Which specifiers, tenders or markets have asked for EPDs in the past year?
  • What did your last EPD cost and how long did it take?
  • Which programme operator do your markets accept?
  • Who would see the sales benefit, and do they know about this project?
Objections

The objections that come up most, with answers

"EC3 is free."EC3 is a good EPD database for procurement. A permit, LEED v5 credit or national limit needs a whole-building assessment to a defined method, which EC3 does not produce.A compliant report from the same model; the scheme list

Battlecard

Against an AI-first EPD startup

When you meet them

A mid-size construction product manufacturer in DACH, the Nordics or the US, starting an EPD programme, with an AI-led pitch and startup pricing in front of them.

What they say

"AI turns your spreadsheets and invoices into verified EPDs in weeks."

Where they are strong

A modern story, hands-on service, introductory pricing, and in Norway an EPD Global grant.

How to win

  • Ask about the range. The cost per EPD across fifty products matters more than the first one.
  • Ask where EPDs will be published. One Click LCA publishes through eight programme operators.
  • Show what happens after publication. Designers find the product inside One Click LCA with real data, and usage analytics show who used it.
  • Show the verification record. Use a named customer with a similar product range.

Landmine to set

"Ask them how many of their EPDs have been verified by your market's programme operator, and how long the last ten took."
