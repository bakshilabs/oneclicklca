Packaging

Four tiers sold per market

Design and construction licences climb from a self-serve Business licence to three sales-led tiers. Every tier is sold against a local compliance bundle, so the market's regulation decides what the buyer gets.

Self-serve

Business

"Buy online, reliable LCA for compliance and certification." €3,030 a year named or €5,180 concurrent per market bundle.

Sales-led

Designer

Compliance module, Materials Compass, Carbon Designer 3D and the BIM integrations.

Most popular

Expert Pack

Private datasets, plausibility checker, life cycle costing, biodiversity tool and an account manager.

Firm-wide

Power Pack

Internal verification, custom templates and generative design for firms scaling LCA across offices.

What reviewers say about packaging. Reviewers on G2 and Capterra complain about licence gating, user management and paying a year upfront. The product lead for Revit told a forum user that mapping suggestions depend on "the relevant license feature". When a feature a user expects sits in a higher tier, the tier has to be explained before the user finds the gap.

Four things I would fix in the first quarter

Proof points that disagree

The same claims appear with different numbers across the site and press boilerplate.

ClaimFigures in use
Standards and methods140+, 100+, 80+; the compliance page shows about 98 cards

People170, 180+, 200+, 300+, 330+

One dated proof point sheet, owned by product marketing and used by web, sales and partners, fixes this in weeks.

Two brands, one buyer question

Since September 2025 the group sells One Click LCA and SimaPro. The CEO describes the split as "the ability to go deep with SimaPro, and the ability to go fast on One Click LCA". Both include product LCA and EPD tools.

Sellers need a rule for which buyer gets which product, and SimaPro resellers need to know the rule will not take their customers.

A revenue story for manufacturers

The manufacturing pages have moved from "create an EPD" to "get discovered, get specified, get selected". That reaches a commercial director, a different buyer from the sustainability manager who commissions EPDs.

This buyer needs its own message, evidence from customers and a business case written in sales terms.

A missing public sector story

Ireland now requires whole life carbon assessments on public projects above €5 million, Canada has a federal standard and green public procurement is being revised in the EU. Public clients appear only inside case studies.

A public sector page and a procurement-led message would give sellers something to send these buyers.
