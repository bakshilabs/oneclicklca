# Interview plan: Senior Product Marketing Manager, One Click LCA

Drafted 23 October 2026 from `job-description.txt` and the CV and notes in the Populous repository.

## The case to make

Arpan has spent 18 years as a buyer and power user of whole life carbon tools inside architecture and engineering firms. He sat on the Foster + Partners whole life carbon leadership team, built early design carbon tools, spoke on a One Click LCA Bootcamp panel in 2023 and organised the London LCA Forum in 2022. He has sold advisory services worth more than £10 million a year, launched a new service line and now runs sales and marketing for Atlas Learn.

The job asks for 7+ years of product marketing in software. Arpan does not hold that title. The interview material should answer the gap with work product: show the positioning, launch and enablement work done for One Click LCA itself, at the standard they would expect in month three. Every page maps to a line of the job description.

A password-protected static site in the same build as the Populous site (shared nav, light and dark, small radii, plain-noun titles), with One Click LCA's own accent colour in place of `#1c19b5`. Pages in nav order:

## The presentation

A 15 to 20 minute walk-through, likely in a hiring manager or panel round:
1. Who buys One Click LCA and why now (Buyer Research, Regulation Map). 4 minutes.
2. Where to win next (Segment Priorities, Competitive Landscape). 4 minutes.
3. What to say (Positioning). 4 minutes.
4. How to launch and sell it (Launch Plan, Sales Enablement, Win Rate Model). 5 minutes.
5. How I work (AI Workflow, 30/60/90). 3 minutes.

Prepare short spoken answers for: the missing product marketing title, a launch that underperformed, how to measure positioning, pricing pressure from free tools like EC3, and why leave practice for a vendor.

## Build order

1. Regulation Map and Competitive Landscape (research heavy, parallel agents, everything sourced).
2. Buyer Research (needs Arpan's interview notes; draft personas first from his experience).
3. Segment Priorities and Positioning (depend on 1 and 2).
4. Launch Plan, Sales Enablement, Win Rate Model.
5. CV, cover letter, AI Workflow, 30/60/90, Overview, password page.
