First 90 Days

The first month is for learning what the CRM, the sellers and the customers already know. The second is for agreeing priorities. The third is for shipping a launch and a kit that sellers use. Each month ends with something product, sales and marketing can review.

Days 1 to 30

Listen

  • Meet product leadership, the Lead Product Marketing role, the VP Marketing, sales leadership, enablement and two regional sales teams.
  • Shadow ten sales calls across construction and manufacturing, including two lost deals.
  • Interview ten customers and five lost prospects in the segments this role owns.
  • Pull win rate, cycle length and loss reasons from Salesforce, and note where the fields are empty.
  • Read the roadmap and the last six months of release notes.

Output

A findings memo: what buyers say, where deals are lost, what the data can and cannot show.

Days 31 to 60

Decide

  • Score sub-segments with product and sales and agree the top three.
  • Agree the launch tiering rule and apply it to the next two quarters of releases.
  • Draft the message house for the priority segments and start testing it in calls.
  • Propose the CRM fields for alternative, trigger and loss reason with revenue operations.
  • Agree a one-page proof point sheet with web, brand and sales.

Output

A product marketing plan for the next two quarters, signed by product and sales.

Days 61 to 90

Ship

  • Run the next tier one or tier two launch end to end with the new kit.
  • Release battlecards for the two alternatives that appear most in lost deals.
  • Put the business case calculator in sellers' hands and train them on it.
  • Brief two reseller or association partners with the same kit.
  • Set the baseline for win rate in priority segments.

Output

A launched release with adoption tracking, and enablement that sellers use without help.

Working principles

How I would approach the job

Evidence before opinion

Every positioning choice cites a buyer interview, a deal or a number. When evidence is missing, the first task is collecting it.

Fewer, better launches

Most monthly releases get a good release note. The effort goes to the few launches that move revenue in named segments.

Material that works without me

A battlecard or business case is finished when a seller or reseller has used it on a call and won.

One number per launch, agreed in advance

Adoption and pipeline targets are set with sales before release, so results can be judged against something.
