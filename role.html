Operating rhythm

How I would run the job week to week

  • Two conversations with users or buyers, recorded and tagged.
  • Review three sales calls, one of them lost or stalled.
  • Answer the sales channel's open questions in one shared thread.
  • Check launch readiness for anything due in the next eight weeks.
  • Win and loss summary by segment and named alternative.
  • Update the regulation calendar with new dates and drafts.
  • Refresh one battlecard and one business case.
  • Run one partner training session.
  • Re-score sub-segments and set launch priorities with product and sales.
  • Review positioning: which messages won and which stalled.
  • Audit proof points across the website and sales decks.
  • Plan the next quarter's launches and campaigns per region.

    For the panel

    Questions I would ask

    Where does product marketing report after the 2026 leadership changes, and which metric is the role judged by?
    Which product line and segments would this role own: design and construction, manufacturing, data products or the shared SimaPro story?
    Which segment is growing fastest, and which is the company most worried about?
    How is win rate measured today? Are loss reasons and named competitors recorded in the CRM?
    How will One Click LCA and SimaPro be positioned for buyers who could use either?

    Which launch in the past year went better than expected, and which went worse?
