Reading the listing

The Role

The listing names eight duties. Read against what One Click LCA has done since 2023, they describe one job: make a fast-growing, regulation-driven portfolio easy to choose, easy to sell and easy to measure, one launch and one segment at a time.

Sources: the Arc.dev copy of the listing, oneclicklca.com, One Click LCA press releases from 2023 to 2026. Inferences are marked as such.

Working relationships

Product marketing has no team of its own to command. Its work only lands through other teams, so most of the job is agreeing priorities with each of them and returning something useful to each.

Operating rhythm

How I would run the job week to week

Every week

  • Two conversations with users or buyers, recorded and tagged.
  • Review three sales calls, one of them lost or stalled.
  • Answer the sales channel's open questions in one shared thread.
  • Check launch readiness for anything due in the next eight weeks.

Every month

  • Win and loss summary by segment and named alternative.
  • Update the regulation calendar with new dates and drafts.
  • Refresh one battlecard and one business case.
  • One partner training session.

Every quarter

  • Re-score sub-segments and set launch priorities with product and sales.
  • Positioning review: which messages won, which stalled.
  • Proof point audit across the website and sales decks.
  • Plan the next quarter's launches and campaigns per region.
Success

What good looks like at six and twelve months

Six months

  • Every launch has a tier, named target segments and a 90-day adoption goal agreed before release.
  • A battlecard exists for each alternative that appears in more than one lost deal in ten.
  • Loss reasons are recorded on most closed deals, so win rate can be read by segment.
  • One proof point set is used across the website, decks and partner kits.

Twelve months

  • Win rate improved in the two priority segments, against the baseline set in the first quarter.
  • A launch calendar runs ahead of the EPBD, CPR and national deadlines, with partners briefed before each date.
  • Sellers and resellers can deliver the business case without product marketing on the call.
  • A clear buyer-by-product account of One Click LCA and SimaPro is in use by both sales teams.
