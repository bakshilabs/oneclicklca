# Writing and content rules

The site is shown on Arpan's screen during the interview and then shared with the One Click LCA interviewer, who may pass it to colleagues. Every page must read well to someone at One Click LCA who opens it later without Arpan there.

## Who is reading
- A One Click LCA employee who knows the company, the product and the numbers better than any public source does.
- Treat what we found as an outside reading from public sources. Say what it is based on and what would confirm it.
- Do not tell them how their company works. Say what the public sources show, then what I would check, ask or do.
- Write in the first person (I) for Arpan. Call the company One Click LCA. Do not write "the panel", "he", "the interviewer" or stage directions.

## How to write
- Plain words and short sentences, usually under 20 words. One idea per sentence.
- Headings name the topic: "Countries with a rule today", "Where lost deals go", "Launch timeline". They are not claims, slogans or jokes.
- Ledes are one or two plain sentences that say what the page contains and what it is based on.
- Prefer numbers, dates, names and sources to adjectives.
- Mark judgement as judgement: "My estimate", "Placeholder figures", "To check with your data".
- British spelling. Full sentences in body copy.

## Do not write
- Punch lines, closing twists or a final sentence that sums up what was just said.
- "Not X, but Y", "X, not Y", "rather than" contrasts, or "it's not about X, it's about Y".
- Em dashes. Use a full stop or a comma.
- Rhetorical questions, "here's the thing", "the real question", "the short answer".
- Hype or filler: powerful, seamless, unlock, game-changing, crucial, key (as an adjective), robust, leverage, journey, landscape, ecosystem, deep dive, toughest, winning, the whole company can use, done once, worked example.
- Lists of three for rhythm. Use as many items as there are.
- Absolute claims about One Click LCA's internals, people, revenue or strategy beyond what a cited source says.
- Information about named individuals other than the CEO, or anything taken from personal LinkedIn profiles.

## Message lines
Example messaging (taglines, promises, objection replies) is part of the work and may stay persuasive. Label it as a draft to test: "Draft message to test".

## Showing on screen
- Anything important must be visible on the page without hovering. Tooltips only add detail.
- Each chart has a one-sentence plain reading under its title.
- Placeholder figures in models are labelled as placeholders, with a line inviting the reader to enter their own.
