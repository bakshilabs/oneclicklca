What to say to whom

Positioning

The homepage promises "Do more LCAs and EPDs, faster". That speaks to the practitioner who runs the tool. The people who sign want a permit, a credit, a bid or a specification. This page keeps the company's platform story and adds a message for each buyer, with the proof each one needs.

Proposals for discussion. Every message here would be tested in sales calls and campaigns before it replaced anything live.

Where it stands

How the story has moved since 2023

2023 to 2024

Category leader

"The world's number one construction LCA and EPD software." The claim rests on database size and compliance coverage, aimed at sustainability practitioners.

2025

Automation and compliance as growth

"AI-powered end-to-end sustainability platform." The October 2025 launch framed regulation as a growth opportunity: "Compliance is no longer optional."

Since September 2025

The largest LCA platform

"The world's largest LCA platform, delivering impact, compliance, and growth." SimaPro widens the story from construction to every industry.

Reading. The platform story is strong for investors, analysts and enterprise buyers. Below that level each buyer needs its own reason in its own terms. Manufacturers are the clearest case: the site now says "Win more bids with EPDs", which speaks to a commercial director, while the product pages still speak to the sustainability manager.

Positioning statement

A statement the whole company can use

For design, construction and manufacturing firms that have to prove the carbon of their buildings and products, One Click LCA is the LCA platform that turns each market's rules into a finished, verifiable result in the least time. It holds the largest construction LCA database, covers more than a hundred standards in one model and automates EPDs from data to publication. Firms use it to win permits, certifications, bids and specifications in every country they work in.

The statement names the buyer, the job, the outcome and three proofs. Each segment message below starts from it.

Testing

How I would test a message before changing the site

  1. Write two or three versions of the promise for one segmentFor example, for manufacturers: "Get specified more often" against "Verified EPDs in days".
  2. Run them in discovery calls firstSellers open with one version for two weeks and the other for two weeks, and record the buyer's response in a CRM field.
  3. Then in paid and outbound campaignsSame audience list, split by message. Measure meetings booked and opportunities created, not clicks.
  4. Keep what moves opportunitiesChange the website and the deck only after both tests point the same way.
