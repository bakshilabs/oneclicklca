Summary

The case in four parts

The meeting pages follow these four points in order.

  1. Disclosure in 2028

    From 1 January 2028, every new EU building over 1,000 m² must disclose its life cycle carbon. Most of the design teams this affects have never bought an LCA tool.

    The 2028 Buyer

  2. The alternative to buying

    A first-time buyer can hire a consultant for one project, or wait. I expect no decision to be the most common loss in these segments. A loss reason in the CRM would show whether that is right.

    The 2028 Buyer

  3. Price

    Value for money is the lowest Capterra sub-score, at 4.0 of 5. Support scores 4.9. Setting a consultant's fees against the licence cost gives a first-time buyer a reason to start this year.

    The 2028 Buyer

  4. A first launch

    I would propose a tier one launch in 2027 for first-time buyers in Germany, Italy and Spain, and for global engineering firms. Sellers would get a business case kit. The launch would be judged on win rate and on the share of deals lost to no decision in those segments.
