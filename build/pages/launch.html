    Worked example

    EU 2028 Launch Plan

    A tier one launch built around the EU life cycle GWP method, timed to the national roadmaps due on 1 January 2027 and aimed at first-time buyers before the 1 January 2028 deadline.

    Goal

    Turn the 2028 deadline into new logos in large EU markets with no national LCA habit. The adoption target is agreed with sales before release, for example new Business and Designer licences in Germany, Italy and Spain in the first two quarters.

    Priority segments

    Chosen with the Segment Model using compliance weights.

    • German designers buying for the first time
    • Italian and Spanish designers
    • Global engineering firms standardising one EU method

    Core message

    "The EU method, done once, accepted everywhere you build."

    The proof is the EU method on the same model as national rules and certifications, an EPC-ready report and local support.
