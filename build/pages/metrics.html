The listing asks for business judgment about what positioning will do to a metric. That needs three things: a model of how the metric responds, the CRM fields to measure it, and a rule for which changes product marketing can claim. This page sets out all three.

Product marketing levers

Through the business case and cost of doing nothing
Through battlecards and positioning against named rivals

Why no decision gets its own lever. In a category where many buyers can still hire a consultant or wait for the rule, the largest loss is often to no decision. A business case that prices the wait moves more deals than a better battlecard. Most CRMs record these as "closed lost, other", so the first change is a loss reason that separates them.

Metric tree

What product marketing owns, shares and influences

Owns

  • Win rate against each named alternative in priority segments
  • Launch adoption at 30, 60 and 90 days against agreed targets
  • Share of closed deals with a recorded loss reason and alternative
  • Seller and partner certification on each tier one launch

Shares with sales

  • Overall win rate and sales cycle length
  • No-decision rate
  • Expansion from Business to Designer and Expert Pack
  • Partner-sourced pipeline

Influences

  • Pipeline from campaigns, through message choice and segment lists
  • Demo to opportunity conversion
  • Web shop conversion, through pricing page and packaging clarity
  • Retention, through what customers expect when they buy
Attribution

How to claim a change honestly

  1. Set the baseline firstRecord two quarters of win rate by segment and alternative before changing positioning, or use the previous year where the data exists.
  2. Change one segment at a timeRoll a new message or kit into one priority segment and keep a comparable segment as a control for a quarter.
  3. Compare deals that used the material with those that did notWithin the same segment and quarter, so seasonality and pricing affect both.
  4. Report the range, not a single numberSmall samples per segment make win rate noisy. A change of a few points needs a few hundred closed deals before it is reliable.
