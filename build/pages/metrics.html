    Simple model: recovered losses become wins at the same contract value. It ignores longer-term expansion.

    No decision gets its own lever. Many buyers can still hire a consultant or wait for the rule, so the largest loss is often to no decision, and a business case that prices the wait moves more of those deals than a better battlecard. Most CRMs record these as "closed lost, other", so the first change is a loss reason that separates them.
