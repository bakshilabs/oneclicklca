My questions

Eight questions about the role

First three, if time is shortThe rest, in the follow-up note if we run out of time
  1. The role

    Where does this role report, and which measure will it be judged on?

    The listing places the role in Product. The measure decides where I spend my first quarter.

  2. Launches

    Which launch in the past year went better than expected, and which went worse?

    I would like to learn from recent launches before I suggest any change to how launches are sized.

  3. Measurement

    Are loss reasons and named competitors recorded on opportunities today?

    If they are, I can read win rate by segment from the start. If they are not, adding them would be one of my first requests.

  4. Markets

    Which segment is growing fastest, and which concerns you most?

    The answers would change which segments I look at first.

  5. Products

    How do One Click LCA and SimaPro divide manufacturers who could use either?

    Both brands sell to manufacturers, and sellers need a clear rule for the first call.

  6. The role

    Which product line and segments would this role cover?

    The February 2026 listing covered manufacturing. The answer decides who I interview first.

  7. Partners

    What share of new revenue comes through resellers and partners, and in which countries?

    Material for partners has to work without me on the call, so I would plan it differently.

  8. Partners

    Which green building councils and EPD programme operators work with One Click LCA on launches today?

    I would plan launches with the bodies that already work with One Click LCA.
