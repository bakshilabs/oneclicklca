Part 1 · Messaging framework

Target buyer and trigger moment

I picked one buyer: the commercial director at a mid-sized construction product manufacturer that sells into the Nordic countries and has no LCA team. This person owns the sales number and the budget. The sustainability or quality manager runs the tool and is the internal champion.

Buyer

Commercial director, mid-sized Nordic product manufacturer

Company
Roughly 100 to 1,000 staff, one to four plants, often family or private equity owned. My estimate of the core of the market.
Range
Dozens to a few hundred products in a handful of families, such as windows, insulation, precast, flooring or façade systems.
EPDs today
A few consultant-made EPDs for flagship products. Most of the range has none.
Sells through
Distributors, contractors and specifications written by architects and engineers.
Measured on
Revenue, margin and specification share in the home and export markets.
Worry
Paying for EPDs that sit on a shelf, and losing projects to a competitor that looks greener on paper.
Trigger moment

A specification is lost to a competitor with an EPD

  1. A design team the sales team has been tracking models the building in a carbon tool, as the national rules require.
  2. The tool has no EPD for this manufacturer's product, so the model uses the national generic value with a safety margin added.
  3. A competing product with its own EPD shows a lower figure and goes into the specification.
  4. The commercial director hears about it from a distributor or a contractor after the decision.

Why Q1 2027. From 1 January 2028, new buildings over 1,000 m² in every EU country must disclose life-cycle carbon. The buildings that report in 2028 are being designed during 2027, so their product choices are made during the campaign.

Part 1 · Draft to test

Positioning statement

For commercial directors at mid-sized Nordic construction product manufacturers without an LCA team, One Click LCA EPD Generator is the way to get every product in the range counted at its own verified carbon figure in the design tools where specifications are decided. It produces EPDs with a pre-verified method at a fixed price per product, publishes them through the programme operators each market accepts and shows which projects use them. Consultant EPDs arrive one product at a time and leave the rest of the range on generic values.

Part 1 · Draft to test

Core narrative

The story runs in five beats. Each campaign asset tells all five or starts at one of them.

  1. Carbon is calculated at design stageSweden, Denmark, Finland and Norway already require a carbon calculation for new buildings. From 2028 every EU country requires a disclosure. Designers model the building before they fix the specification.
  2. Missing data is filled with a penaltyWhen a product has no EPD, the tool uses a national generic value. These values are set high on purpose. Sweden adds 25% to its generic data.
  3. The loss is silentThe product looks worse than it is, a competitor with an EPD looks better, and the decision is made before the sales team hears about the project.
  4. The range can be covered without an LCA teamEPD Generator turns plant and product data into verified EPDs at a fixed price per product, and publishes them where designers will find them.
  5. Start with what customers specify mostThe first product families to cover are the ones that appear most often in customers' projects in 2027, while those designs are still open.

Part 3

Rationale

Assumptions

  • Designers using One Click LCA pick a product-specific EPD when one is available.
  • Generic values make most products look worse than their real figure. A product worse than the generic value gains nothing, so the gap check screens for this.
  • The commercial director holds the budget and reacts to lost specifications more than to compliance.
  • Most mid-sized Nordic manufacturers have EPDs for a few flagship products only.
  • One Click LCA's usage data can show generic-data use by product category and country. The gap check and the placeholder proof depend on this.

Validating the messaging

  • Eight to ten interviews with commercial directors at target manufacturers, testing the trigger moment and the H1 against "faster, compliant, cheaper".
  • A win and loss review of 2026 EPD Generator deals for the reasons buyers gave.
  • A paid LinkedIn test of two headlines to sales and commercial roles at Nordic manufacturers, measured on gap-check requests per visitor.
  • Two weeks of sales calls using the narrative, with sellers logging which beat moved the conversation.

Extending to other channels

  • Sales: the gap check becomes the first-call agenda, run on each prospect's own product list.
  • Associations: co-hosted webinars and member offers, following the Aluminium Danmark partnership.
  • Designers: content showing architects how generic values inflate their results, so they ask suppliers for EPDs.
  • Events: a manufacturer customer on stage at the Carbon Experts Summit.
  • Email: a nurture sequence for gap-check leads, one product family at a time.

Method

Research workflow

I ran four research subagents in parallel, one per question, while I drafted the structure. Each wrote a sourced research file. I then chose the angle, wrote the framework and wireframe, and checked the page against the site's writing rules and accessibility tests.
