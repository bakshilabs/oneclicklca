### Observations for a PMM (my analysis, not sourced claims)
- Proof-point numbers are inconsistent across pages: 140+ vs 80+ vs 100+ standards; 500k vs 300k datasets; 170/180/200+ staff; 20+ vs about 32 integrations. The press-room boilerplate is stale. This is a quick-win messaging-hygiene task.
- The narrative has moved from "embodied carbon for buildings" to "**automation, AI and compliance-as-growth**" across construction and manufacturing. After the SimaPro deal it is moving further, to a cross-industry LCA platform. The two-brand architecture (One Click LCA vs SimaPro) is not yet resolved publicly.
- Manufacturing is shifting from "make an EPD" to "**EPD-to-revenue**" (usage analytics, specification opportunities, Get specified). This is a classic PMM opportunity.
- Packaging is a "good, better, best" ladder (Designer/Expert/Power) plus a self-serve Business tier sold per market compliance bundle. That makes local regulation the unit of packaging.
- Launch rhythm is regulation-driven, and in 2026 releases have skewed toward partnerships and data rather than product. The Oct 2025 bundle was the last big product-launch press release I found.
