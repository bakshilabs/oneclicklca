# What happens when a construction product has no EPD: national generic and default values

Research date: 11 November 2026. Web research, primary sources (regulators, official journals, national databases, government-commissioned reports) wherever they could be reached.

Status key: **VERIFIED** = read in a primary or government source on the date above. **VERIFIED (secondary official)** = read in an official or government-commissioned document from another body (for example the Norwegian regulator describing the Finnish database), but not on the issuing body's own page. **UNVERIFIED** = only seen in vendor, trade press or draft material, or could not be confirmed.

## 2. Country notes

### 2.1 Sweden

**Generic values and the 25% uplift (VERIFIED)**
- Boverket's climate database page: "Climate data is conservatively set, i.e. approximately 25 percent higher than the average value." Average values are published separately as "typical values". "If generic climate data is used in the climate declaration, it must be based on data from Boverket's climate database." Boverket recommends specific data for purchased products. Energy and fuel data are not conservatively set.
- Page published 26 Feb 2021, last modified 21 Jan 2026; database version 02.07.000 (21 Jan 2026).
- Source: https://www.boverket.se/en/start/laws-and-regulations/climate-declaration/climate-database/
- Swedish page (same content): https://www.boverket.se/sv/klimatdeklaration/klimatdatabas/om-klimatdatabas/
- 2026 update notes: https://www.boverket.se/contentassets/eb39a37752af4f74a61ccf92ac21cd5b/uppdateringar-i-boverkets-klimatdatabas-2026.pdf

**Current climate declaration rules (VERIFIED)**
- Act on Climate Declarations for Buildings (2021:787), applying to permits applied for from 1 Jan 2022. Covers the construction stage A1-A5. Generic data from Boverket's database or third-party verified EPDs may be used. Boverket's 2026 report confirms these existing rules and proposes that the Act cease to apply once the EPBD rules take over.

**Extended declaration expected 2028 (VERIFIED as proposal; adoption UNVERIFIED)**
- Boverket Report 2026:27, "Regulation on life-cycle GWP and limit values for the climate impact of buildings in accordance with the EPBD, Part 1" (Swedish original May 2026; English translation published September 2026).
  - Life-cycle GWP calculated and reported from 1 Jan 2028 for new buildings over 1,000 m2, from 1 Jan 2030 for all other new buildings; limit values from 1 Jan 2030, covering A1-A5.
  - Proposed 2030 limits (kg CO2e/m2 GFA, GWP-GHG): single-family houses 130 m2 or more 130; under 130 m2 150; multi-dwelling blocks 235; offices, schools, preschools, primary care 250; accommodation 240; logistics and trade over 1,000 m2 190; indoor pools 455; shelters 1,500; other buildings 295.
  - Coverage ratio of at least 90% "including the use of default values"; where coverage is below 100%, "the climate impact shall be adjusted upwards" to represent the whole building.
  - "Boverket will provide conservative default values for certain building elements", described as transitional, to be phased out. "The conservative upwards adjustment creates an incentive to carry out climate calculations for these elements."
  - Existing GWP-GHG generic data "can continue to be used"; the database must add GWP-total values. Boverket "plans to publish an updated climate database in early 2027".
  - Source: https://www.boverket.se/globalassets/publikationer/dokument/2026/regulation-on-life-cycle-gwp-and-limit-values-for-the-climate-impact-of-buildings-part-1.pdf ; landing page https://www.boverket.se/en/start/publications/2026/ny-sidregulation-on-life-cycle-gwp-and-limit-values-for-the-climate-impact-of-buildings/
  - Swedish original: https://www.boverket.se/globalassets/publikationer/dokument/2026/rapport-regler-om-livscykel-gwp-och-gransvarden-for-byggnaders---del-1.pdf
- UNVERIFIED: whether the government has adopted the proposal; whether the 25% uplift will be kept at the same level for the new GWP-total values. Note: one search summary gave the report number as 2026:16; the report itself says 2026:27.

### 2.2 Finland

**CO2data.fi generic values and the 1.2 factor (VERIFIED, secondary official)**
- Syke describes the database as "average data for the most common and typical building products", based on public sources such as EPDs, with no individual commercial products. Page published 5 Feb 2025, updated 31 Aug 2026. https://www.syke.fi/en/environmental-data/calculators-and-tools/co2data-service/emissions-database-building-construction
- The 20% conservative factor is stated in:
  - Norwegian regulator DiBK guidance (2023): Finland gives an average value and "en konservativ verdi som skal brukes i forskriftssammenheng. Den konservative verdien er 20 % høyere enn gjennomsnittsverdien" (a conservative value for regulatory use, 20% higher than the average).
  - Nordic Council of Ministers report us2024:428 (co-authored by IVL and Syke, the CO2data operator): "an addition of 20% was introduced in Finland and 25% in Sweden ... On average, the use of EPDs will result in a discount of 20 or 25%". https://pub.norden.org/us2024-428/us2024-428.pdf
  - Aalborg University BUILD Report 2023:16 (for the Danish authority): Finland "anvender en sikkerhedsfaktor på 1,20 for en typisk byggevare".
  - Helsinki city report (2023) on Hankeikkuna: "CO2data.fi päästötiedossa on mukana päästötiedon konservatiivisuuskerroin 1,2".
- Ministry of the Environment webinar slides (M. Tiainen, 24 Oct 2025) list the EU data hierarchy and note "Geneeristen arvojen määrittämisessä voidaan käyttää konservatiivisuuskerrointa" (a conservativeness factor may be used in setting generic values). https://tiedostot.rakennustieto.fi/281025/Tianen_Raja-arvot%20ja%20ilmastoselvityksen%20tilannekatsaus.pdf
- UNVERIFIED: the 1.2 factor was not visible on co2data.fi or the Syke pages read; whether it continues unchanged in the 2026 database version.

**Building Act carbon limits (VERIFIED, with caveats)**
- Building Act 751/2023 entered into force 1 Jan 2025. Climate declaration and building product list required for new buildings whose permit is applied for from 1 Jan 2026 (Decree 1027/2024 sets the method). Detached houses excluded.
- Government Decree 2/2026 on carbon footprint limit values approved 8 Jan 2026, applies to permit applications from 9 Jan 2026; limits "will become stricter at the beginning of 2029". Up to 5% overrun allowed in specially difficult cases. https://rt.fi/en/tiedotteet-ja-uutiset/2026/01/raja-arvot-rakennusten-hiilijalanjaljelle-voimaan-9-1/
- Limit values in the notified draft (Ministry slides, Oct 2025), kg CO2e/m2/a, first period / tightened period: terraced houses 16.0 / 13.0; blocks of flats 16.0 / 12.0; offices and health centres 20.0 / 17.0; commercial and cultural 22.0 / 18.0; accommodation and care 25.0 / 22.0; education and day care 20.0 / 16.0; sports halls 21.0 / 18.0; hospitals 29.0 / 28.0; warehouses, transport buildings, pools and ice rinks over 1,000 m2 24.0 / 21.0.
- UNVERIFIED: that the final decree kept these figures. The draft dated the tightening 2028; the final decree moved it to 2029. The government press release (valtioneuvosto.fi) returned HTTP 403.

### 2.3 Denmark

**Generic data (GenDK) and safety factor (VERIFIED)**
- BR18 s. 297(7): materials (A1-A3, C3-C4, D) are calculated with "det generiske datagrundlag i bilag 2, tabel 7"; EPDs to EN 15804 (project-specific, product-specific or average) may be used instead. Table 7 was replaced by BEK nr. 271 of 11 Mar 2025, in force 1 Jul 2025. https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/271/dan/pdf
- Method for the new Danish generic data (Kragh and Birgisdottir, "Udvikling af dansk generisk LCA-data", BUILD Report 2023:16, Aalborg University; lists revised Jan and Aug 2024): A1-A3 = maximum EPD value x 1.1 where a group has 5 or fewer products; 75th percentile x 1.1 where it has more than 5; biogenic negative values x 0.9. C3-C4 = maximum x 1.1; module D = maximum x 0.9. The stated aim is a "retvisende konservativt niveau" and that "det generelt ikke bør være en fordel at anvende generiske data fremfor produktspecifikke EPD'er". https://vbn.aau.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/738580344/Udvikling_af_dansk_generisk_LCA-data.pdf
- Effect: for groups with 5 or fewer EPDs, the generic value sits above every known EPD; for larger groups it sits at 10% above the 75th percentile. There is no single "percent above average" figure; it depends on spread within each group.

**Limit values from 1 Jul 2025 (VERIFIED, BEK 271/2025 s. 298)**
- A1-A3, B4, B6, C3-C4, kg CO2e/m2/yr over 50 years: summer houses under 150 m2 4.0; summer houses 150 m2 or more 6.7; farmhouses, detached, terraced, linked and semi-detached houses 6.7; multi-storey housing, offices, retail, warehouses 7.5; other buildings 8.0. Construction process A4-A5 capped at 1.5. Voluntary low-emission class: 3.2 / 5.4 / 5.4 / 6.1 / 6.4, with A4-A5 at most 1.1.
- Applies to all new buildings except unheated buildings under 50 m2, transportable structures and temporary pavilions (with some extension exemptions).
- UNVERIFIED: secondary sources quote a weighted average of 7.1 and tightening to about 6.4 (2027) and 5.8 (2029); not in the order read.

### 2.4 Norway

**Safety factor on generic data (VERIFIED)**
- DiBK, "Veileder for utarbeidelse av klimagassregnskap, TEK17 s. 17-1", dated 1 Jul 2023, revised 18 Aug 2023, s. 5.2: "Generiske utslippsverdier gis et påslag på 25 % i klimagassregnskapet, med mindre slikt påslag allerede er innbakt i verdien." Generic values may be used only for A1-A3 (guidance tables also apply 25% to generic B2 and B4 values). "Det skal ikke gis 25 % påslag når utslippsverdiene hentes fra en EPD som gjelder for byggevaren. Dette gjelder også når utslippsverdier hentes fra en bransje-EPD." Ready-mix and precast concrete without EPD (including NB publication 37 low-carbon concrete classes) also take the 25%.
- Source: https://www.dibk.no/byggtekniske-omrader/veileder-om-klimagassregnskap/Veileder%20for%20utarbeidelse%20av%20klimagassregnskap_august%202023.pdf
- TEK17 s. 17-1 (climate gas account for materials, NS 3720 method) applies to residential blocks and commercial buildings; in force 1 Jul 2022 with a one-year transition.
- Pending: DiBK consultation note of 5 Feb 2026 proposes extending s. 17-1 and a new s. 17-2 with limit values (kg CO2e/m2 BTA); DiBK assumed entry into force no earlier than during 2026. https://hoering.dibk.no/Hoering/LastNedVedlegg/38076
- UNVERIFIED: final adoption and in-force date of s. 17-2; whether the 25% will change.

### 2.5 Iceland

**25% safety factor (VERIFIED in official proposal; final guidance UNVERIFIED)**
- Regulation 383/2024 amending Building Regulation 112/2012: LCA required for new permit-requiring buildings in consequence classes 2 and 3; HMS to issue method guidance; results must cite traceable data. Takes effect 1 Sep 2025. https://files.reglugerd.is/pdf/0383-2024/current
- Government working group, "Samræmd aðferðafræði lífsferilsgreininga fyrir íslenskar byggingar" (January 2024, published on the government consultation portal): average data "+25%" for A1-A3, A4, A5 and C1-C4, explained as "varfærnisstuðli á losun sem er +25% hærra en meðaltal" both to avoid optimistic results and "til að hvetja til notkunar á nákvæmari gögnum eins og EPD blöðum". The same document's Nordic comparison table lists conservative factors: Finland 20%, Norway 25%, Sweden 25%, Iceland 25%, Denmark "under review". https://samradapi.island.is/api/Documents/fa1121b7-eacb-ee11-9bc1-005056bcce7e
- UNVERIFIED: that HMS's final published guidance keeps the 25% (only a vendor help page, dated 23 Jun 2025, confirms it is applied in practice).

### 2.6 Netherlands

**NMD category 3 and the 30% surcharge (VERIFIED)**
- Stichting NMD: "A 30% markup factor (factor 1.3) applies to category 3 data." It "accounts for less comprehensive inventory data" and is meant to encourage category 1 (brand-specific) and 2 (sector) declarations. Applied "at the product component level for each environmental indicator"; not applied where module D is negative; not applied to category 3a (energy carriers, external energy infrastructure, fully reusable elements). "The markup factor may be adjusted by Stichting NMD." https://milieudatabase.nl/en/environmental-data-lca/category-3-data/boundary-conditions-for-category-3-environmental-declarations/
- Categories: https://milieudatabase.nl/en/database/categories-of-environmental-declarations-in-the-dutch-environmen/

**MPG (VERIFIED)**
- RVO (checked 25 Sep 2026): until 1 Jul 2026, 0.8 (dwellings) and 1.0 (offices over 100 m2) EUR/m2/yr (Bbl art. 4.159). From 1 Jul 2026, with the revised method (A2 set, 19 impact categories): dwellings 1.6 (no real tightening, method change), offices 1.55 (15% tighter), plus first-time limits for retail (1.85) and other functions (1.8), and new requirements for schools, care and industrial halls (Staatsblad 2025, 432). https://www.rvo.nl/onderwerpen/wetten-en-regels-gebouwen/milieuprestatie-gebouwen-mpg
- Note: MPG is a monetised multi-indicator score (MKI), so the 30% applies to all indicators, not only GWP.

### 2.7 France

**RE2020 default environmental data (VERIFIED)**
- Ministry procedure "Procédure de développement de données environnementales par défaut (DED)", V4, November 2022: "Une DED comporte toujours un coefficient de sécurité".
  - Case 1 (only one FDES/PEP): extrapolated values "auxquelles sont appliquées un coefficient de sécurité de + 100%".
  - Case 2 (several FDES/PEP): mean + 2 or 3 standard deviations, or the sample maximum, depending on sample size, then "+ 30 %". Service life set to the minimum in the sample.
  - Case 3 (generic Ecoinvent/ELCD inventories): "+ 30%".
  - Positive indicators (recycling, reuse) take the minimum less the coefficient; biogenic carbon is exempt from the coefficient.
  - Source: https://rt-re-batiment.developpement-durable.gouv.fr/IMG/pdf/procedure_de_developpement_ded_novembre_2022.pdf
- Combined effect: a case 2 DED is far more than 30% above the mean, because the 30% sits on top of mean plus 2-3 standard deviations. The Danish BUILD report calls the French method "meget konservativ".
- From 1 Jan 2025 the modulation "qui permettait de ne pas pénaliser les projets ayant recours aux données environnementales par défaut" was removed (FFB, 12 Mar 2025), alongside tighter Ic construction thresholds. https://www.ffbatiment.fr/actualites-batiment/actualite-bam/re-2020-nouveaux-seuils-et-assouplissement-de-certaines-dispositions
- Ic construction_max moyen (kg CO2e/m2): houses 640 (2022-24), 530 (2025-27), 475 (2028-30), 415 (2031); collective housing 740, 650, 580, 490 (ministry and AQC tables, via search; VERIFIED secondary).
- UNVERIFIED: the exact name and cap of the removed DED modulation term in the arrêté.

## 3. EU level

### 3.1 EPBD recast and Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/52 (VERIFIED)
- Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/52 of 16 Dec 2025, replacing Annex III of Directive (EU) 2024/1275 (Union framework for national life-cycle GWP calculation). Published OJ L 4 May 2026; enters into force on the twentieth day after publication (24 May 2026). Reference study period 50 years. ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/52/oj (text read via an OJ copy: https://refindustry.com/upload/EC_OJ_L_202600052_EN_TXT.pdf ; EUR-Lex itself blocked automated access).
- Disclosure: new buildings over 1,000 m2 useful floor area from 1 Jan 2028; all new buildings from 1 Jan 2030 (EPBD Art. 7(2)).
- Data hierarchy (Annex, point 3): CPR DoPC data "shall be used when available", then compatible ecodesign data. Project-specific, product-specific and sector-average EN 15804 data "may be used only if they are specifically permitted by national legislation".
- Generic data: Member State rules "shall be based on conservative assumptions, so as not to favour generic data on an unfair basis over product-specific data".
- Default values: fill data gaps or simplify; "Member States may set default values with conservative assumptions that encourage the calculation with specific data when available."
- "Member States shall set out clear rules, with a consistent and conservative approach, to generate and update generic data and default values."
- No EU-wide percentage is set. Size of the penalty remains national.

### 3.2 CPR (EU) 2024/3110 (VERIFIED)
- Applies from 8 Jan 2026 (Art. 96). Art. 15(3): the DoPC "shall cover at least" Annex II points (a) to (d) (climate change total, fossil, biogenic, land use) from 8 Jan 2026; points (e)-(m) from 9 Jan 2030; (n)-(s) from 9 Jan 2032. Calculated with free Commission software (Art. 15(2)).
- In practice the obligation bites per product family only from one year after the implementing act making a new harmonised technical specification mandatory (Art. 95(9)); until then the old CPR regime and standards continue for most products. So most products do not yet carry DoPC GWP data.
- Penalties (Art. 92): Member States lay down rules, "effective, proportionate and dissuasive", and must notify the Commission "by 8 December 2026". Art. 92 "shall apply from 8 January 2027". No harmonised fine level; the Commission evaluates fragmentation by 9 Jan 2033.
- Text read via https://lawplayer.com/eu/act/32024R3110 (consolidated copy). ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/3110/oj
- Correction to earlier notes: the 8 Jan 2027 date is when the penalty article applies, not when GWP declaration becomes mandatory.

## 4. Is the default designed to be conservative, and by how much?

| Jurisdiction | Conservative by design | How much above a typical product |
|---|---|---|
| Sweden | Yes | About 25% above product-group average |
| Finland | Yes | 20% above typical value |
| Norway | Yes | 25% above whichever generic source is used |
| Iceland | Yes | 25% above average (proposal) |
| Denmark | Yes | 10% above the 75th percentile, or 10% above the worst EPD for small groups; percentage above average varies by group |
| Netherlands | Yes | 30% above category 3 base data |
| France | Yes, the strongest | +100% (one source), or mean + 2-3 SD then +30% |
| EU 2026/52 | Required for generic data, optional for default values | Not set |

Overall: every jurisdiction reviewed treats a product with no EPD as worse than the typical product. The Nordic rule of thumb is +20% to +25%. The Nordic Council of Ministers report states the logic directly: using an EPD gives "on average ... a discount of 20 or 25%".
