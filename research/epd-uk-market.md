# UK market research: One Click LCA EPD Generator for mid-sized construction product manufacturers

Compiled 11 November 2026 from public web sources only. Target: mid-sized UK construction product manufacturers without an in-house LCA team. Proposed buying unit: commercial director (buyer), sustainability or technical manager (user), managing director (signatory).

Conventions:
- Quotes are verbatim from the page as fetched on 11 November 2026. Page dates are as shown on the page.
- **UNVERIFIED** marks anything taken from a search snippet, a secondary source only, an undated page, or an inference.
- Vendor sources (One Click LCA, Emidat, Ecochain, EPD Hub, consultancies) are flagged as such. Their figures are self-reported.
- Named individuals are left out on purpose. Only company names and job titles appear.

## 2. Evidence that UK specifiers, contractors and clients ask for, prefer or require EPDs

### Government (primary source)
- **Construction Products Reform White Paper (Feb 2026)**, para 10.3.5: "Construction products manufacturers are already being asked by their buyers for product level embodied emissions data, normally in the form of EPDs prepared in line with EN15804 based on life cycle analysis (LCA) methodologies." Para 10.3.6: "the need to produce an EPD for products not covered by a designated standard will remain voluntary." Para 10.3.5 also notes "confusion from manufacturers (particularly in an SME dominated sector) about the best route to present environmental data". Footnote 52 cites the One Click LCA Carbon Experts Report 2025. https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/699c68cad2b9c6ec5b6fbbe8/Construction_Products_Reform_White_Paper.pdf (published 25 or 26 Feb 2026; consultation closed 20 May 2026)
- **MHCLG/DLUHC research summary (7 Jul 2025)**: "One third of materials and products sampled lacked UK based EPDs." and "the cost of producing Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs), hinders the ability of smaller manufacturers to prove their products as lower carbon." Also: "Development of low-cost LCA tools should be explored for use by SMEs". https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/consideration-of-embodied-carbon-in-new-buildings/the-practical-technical-and-economic-impacts-of-measuring-and-reducing-embodied-carbon-in-new-buildings-executive-summary

### Contractors
- **Laing O'Rourke, Supply Chain Sustainability Charter** (PDF metadata 14 Oct 2024): "Supply chain partners will be required to provide EPDs when requested, or the provision of alternative data where this is not yet available." and "Some projects require Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) as a minimum standard - in these instances we will look to prioritise those suppliers that can provide us with the necessary information." https://www.laingorourke.com/media/ewajeqrf/supply-chain-sustainability-charter.pdf. **Best contractor proof point found.**
- **Balfour Beatty, Sustainable Procurement Policy** (signed December 2020): priority to work "with our supply chain partners to report and reduce embodied carbon in materials they supply to us". Does not name EPDs. https://www.balfourbeatty.com/media/319260/prc-pl-0007-sustainable-procurement-policy.pdf
- **Kier**: Responsible Sourcing Guide and procurement policy found, no EPD wording extracted (**UNVERIFIED**). Net zero across operations and supply chain by 2045 (search snippet). https://www.kier.co.uk/media/idmpvaws/kier-responsible-sourcing-guide.pdf
- **Willmott Dixon**: no EPD requirement found. 2024 review: 238 partners (73% of Cat A supply chain) report carbon emissions; target to cut embodied carbon from purchased goods and services 55% by 2030 (search snippet, **UNVERIFIED** exact wording). https://willmottdixon.co.uk/sustainable-development-reviews/now-or-never-2024-sustainability-review/our-2024-performance/brilliant-buildings/materials-supply-chain

### Specifiers and architects (NBS)
- NBS, 24 May 2023: "86% of specifiers consider Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) to be an important aspect" of their decision-making. https://www.thenbs.com/manufacturers/news-and-press/nine-in-ten-architects-want-third-party-certification-for-building-products
- NBS Sustainable Futures, 27 May 2025: "Nearly half of specifiers (46%) and 52% of architects will only select manufacturers" with above-average sustainability credentials, "especially strong when supported by Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs)". Also "environmental performance and embodied carbon data are missing from manufacturers' top five provided data types." https://www.thenbs.com/news-and-press/specifiers-demand-better-embodied-carbon-data-from-manufacturers-to-achieve-net-zero
- NBS Sustainable Futures Report 2026, as reported by PBC Today (29 Oct 2026): "Most respondents (83%) want manufacturers to provide more sustainability performance data" and "43% look for EPDs when selecting products." Secondary source only (**UNVERIFIED** against the NBS report). https://pbctoday.co.uk/news/energy-news/how-can-sustainability-in-construction-be-consistently-measured/167122/

### UKGBC, standards and schemes
- UKGBC Net Zero Whole Life Carbon Roadmap, Stakeholder Action Plan (Material & Product Manufacturers; PDF dated Jul 2023): manufacturers "working towards 40% of their standard product portfolio (in terms of embodied carbon impact) by 2025" and "100% EPD by 2030", "with minimum thresholds and support and subsidies for SMEs". https://ukgbc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Material-and-PM-Stakeholder-Action-Plan-WLCR.pdf
- UKGBC Construction Products Reform Green Paper response (May 2025): "EPDs are already expensive for manufacturers, so this might require additional support" (search snippet, **UNVERIFIED**). https://ukgbc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Construction-Products-Reform-Green-Paper-UKGBC-response-Chap-7-10-2.pdf
- UK Net Zero Carbon Buildings Standard (v1 published 23 Mar 2026, voluntary). NFRC position paper (1 Apr 2026): "The standard aligns with RICS Whole Life Carbon Assessment methodology, which prioritises verified, product-specific EPDs." Without EPDs "generic default values apply". https://www.nfrc.co.uk/resource/uk-net-zero-carbon-need-to-know.html
- BREEAM V7 (launched 30 Sept 2025). Emidat blog (vendor, 28 Aug 2026): Mat 02 credit "is only awarded once the project accumulates 20 EPD points" and "A product-specific EPD is worth three times a sector average." https://www.emidat.com/blog/how-epds-score-in-breeam (point values **UNVERIFIED** against the BREEAM V7 manual)
- RIBA 2030 Climate Challenge and LETI: no direct EPD requirement wording found (**UNVERIFIED**). RIBA Journal Unilin piece describes moving to "materials with improved verified EPDs" (search snippet).

No UK-only figures. https://oneclicklca.com/en/resources/press-release/global-study-97-of-construction-professionals-expect-demand-for-environmental-product-data-to-increase

## 3. UK EPD numbers, programme operators and growth

- **UK total (dated):** ASBP: start of 2021 "360 EPD using EN 15804 for UK produced products" from "over 60 manufacturers and UK trade associations"; start of 2022 "over 450 manufacturer specific EPD from over 80 manufacturers" plus "12 UK Trade Association EPD" (about 25% growth). https://asbp.org.uk/online-briefing-paper/epd-uk-products. **No current (2025 or 2026) UK total found.**
- **Global context:** ConstructionLCA via ASBP: at Jan 2025 "nearly 40,000 verified Environmental Product Declarations (EPD) to EN 15804" registered with programmes; over 180,000 construction EPDs globally estimated. https://asbp.org.uk/asbp-news/constructionlcas-2025-guide-to-epd
- **BRE Global (UK programme operator):** "Over 250 EPDs have already been published" (undated). Two routes: BRE Consultancy, or BRE LINA tool subscription. https://bregroup.com/services/standards/environmental/en-15804-environmental-product-declaration-epd. A June 2026 BRE EPD is numbered 000815 (search snippet; implies a larger cumulative total, **UNVERIFIED**).
- **International EPD System:** at 10,000 EPDs (25 Sept 2024) "the top five countries ... are Italy, Sweden, Spain, Türkiye, and the United Kingdom". https://www.environdec.com/news/milestone-over-10000-epds. In 2025 "a total of 9,395 EPDs were published" (no UK split). https://www.environdec.com/news/epd-development-2025 (23 Dec 2025)
- **EPD Hub:** "6,000th Environmental Product Declaration"; "4,000 were published last year alone"; prices "50% lower than other program operators"; verification "just 2 to 4 weeks" (25 Jun 2026). https://www.epdhub.com/about-epd-hub/press-releases/6000-epds. Became fully independent of One Click LCA, keeping integrations "as a partner" (5 Feb 2026). https://www.epdhub.com/about-epd-hub/press-releases/epd-hub-fully-independent. **UK share not published (UNVERIFIED).** Metsec and MPA asphalt sector EPDs use EPD Hub (HUB- numbers).
- **Circular Ecology** runs a UK EPD programme operator (https://circularecology.com/epd-programme-fees.html).
- **Sector EPDs:** MPA publishes sector EPDs (cement covering "100% of all cement produced in the UK", ready-mixed concrete, precast, asphalt, blocks) mainly via the International EPD System and EPD Hub (search snippets). https://mineralproducts.org/News/2024/release17.aspx

## 4. Typical cost and time of a consultant-made EPD in the UK

- **Circular Ecology (UK consultancy and programme operator; 2026 page):** consultancy median "£19,550", range "£15,350-£26,500"; verification median "£2,330", range "£1,600 to £2,880"; combined consultancy plus verification "approximately £15,610" (2014 survey uplifted to 2026). Effort: "between 5 and 20 person days, with a median of 15 person days" for LCA and EPD; verification "a median of 3.8 person days". https://circularecology.com/how-much-does-it-cost-to-produce-an-epd.html (converted from international surveys, not a UK price list)
- **Programme fees:** Circular Ecology programme, first EPD: registration "£1,000", maintenance "£750", SCRI contribution "£250", total "£2,000". https://circularecology.com/epd-programme-fees.html. EPD Hub: "€ 2,850" for one EPD, "We combine both verification and publishing into the one price"; 10 EPDs €13,000; 20 EPDs €24,000. https://www.epdhub.com/about-epd-hub/pricing
- **Time:** "most organisations can expect the full process to take 3 to 6 months"; verification allow "1 to 2 months for most projects". https://circularecology.com/how-long-does-it-take-to-produce-an-epd.html. UKGBC solution page for Emidat (16 Jan 2026) states manual workflows take "6 to 12 months per EPD" (vendor-supplied). https://ukgbc.org/resources/fast-preverified-environmental-product-data/
- **BRE:** no public price. BRE LINA: ASBP members offered "a 3-month subscription to BRE's LINA tool at a discounted rate" of £2,500, "largely aimed at SMEs looking to create 1 or 2 EPD"; verification, scheme membership and GreenBookLive listing extra (c. 2022, may have expired). https://asbp.org.uk/asbp-news/discounted-epd-fees-members
- One Click LCA: no UK prices published ("Request a quote"). https://oneclicklca.com/en-gb/pricing/product-lca-and-epd-pricing. Older global article cites "average costs of around 15,000 USD" per EPD (5 Mar 2024). https://oneclicklca.com/en-us/resources/case-studies/the-business-case-for-investing-in-epds/

**Planning figure for messaging:** roughly £15,000 to £27,000 and 3 to 6 months per consultant-led EPD, plus £2,000 to £2,900 programme or verification fees. Derived from Circular Ecology and EPD Hub; not a UK market survey.

## 7. Who decides and pays for EPDs at UK manufacturers

- **No UK survey found** that names the role that decides or pays (CPA, BRE, NBS, ASBP searched). **UNVERIFIED** territory.
- The White Paper frames demand as coming from "their buyers" and describes SMEs as confused about routes, which points to a commercial trigger and a technical execution problem.
- Job titles fronting UK EPD stories: Sustainability Director (Genuit), Head of Product & Development (Polypipe Building Services), Technical Data Administrator (Ibstock), Platform Sustainability and Communications Manager (Promat), Sustainability Manager (Hydro), Managing Director Readymix (Holcim UK), roof systems director (Marley, search snippet). Commercial director titles did not appear in any UK EPD announcement found.
- Ibstock "trained its commercial teams to explain EPDs", so sales and commercial teams use the output.
- One Click LCA global article: "it is of course the manufacturer who must pay to develop EPDs" (no role named).
- Inference (**UNVERIFIED**): at mid-sized firms without LCA staff, the sustainability or technical manager usually starts the EPD project, while budget sits with commercial or MD when a named customer or tender asks for it.
