## 1. Marketing and product org

### 1.1 The role itself: Senior Product Marketing Manager (current ad)

Source: [careers.oneclicklca.com/jobs/8478889](https://careers.oneclicklca.com/jobs/8478889-senior-product-marketing-manager-for-a-sustainability-saas) [Verified via Exa crawl]. Also on [Arc.dev](https://arc.dev/remote-jobs/j/one-click-lca-senior-product-marketing-manager-for-a-sustainability-saas-pp3jw85uno).

- **Department: "Product Management"** (not Marketing). The location is Helsinki HQ or the Netherlands and the ad says "Fully Remote". The application deadline was **11 Oct 2026**. The role no longer appears on the careers page, which lists only 3 roles (Engineering Manager, Head of Security, Finance Controller). The hiring process is therefore likely under way.
- What the job involves:
  - talk regularly with users and buyers about their criteria and how they buy
  - **prioritise subsegments for each launch**
  - write positioning and messaging
  - analyse the buyer's "best alternatives, the cost of doing nothing and competitive or substituting solutions"
  - plan and run product and version-upgrade launches
  - train sales channels on the problems solved and the business case
  - **"Deliver improvements in sales win rates for the targeted products and achieve new product launch adoption and sales targets"**
  - contribute to the marketing plans the commercial teams run
- Requirements:
  - 7+ years of PMM experience
  - "engineering-driven, complex products"
  - business acumen to judge "what potential impact it could have on a metric"
  - **"Ability to leverage AI effectively across your workflow"**
  - LCA/EPD familiarity is "an advantage"
  - a "get-things-done" attitude

**Older PMM ads show how the function has evolved**
- **Aug 2024 PMM** ([community post](https://community.oneclicklca.com/t/product-marketing-manager/1509)) [Verified]: required "significant experience in sustainability in the construction or manufacturing industries" and said "Previous experience in marketing or communication roles is not required". At that point the company hired PMMs as domain experts.
- **Feb 2026 PMM, manufacturing product line** ([job 7133388](https://careers.oneclicklca.com/jobs/7133388-product-marketing-manager-for-a-sustainability-saas), posted 22 Feb 2026, now returns 410) [Verified via Exa cache]:
  - Product Management department, fully remote in Europe
  - "help shape and scale the product marketing function... a small but growing team... help structure the PMM function"
  - four pillars: (1) **Roadmap communication**, as "the bridge between the product and the rest of the company"; (2) GTM strategy and execution, including monitoring launch performance; (3) messaging and positioning "for our **manufacturing product line**"; (4) enablement, covering one-pagers, FAQs, pitch decks, **battlecards** and sales enablement sessions
  - also "cross-functional workflows and best practices as the PMM function continues to mature"
- **Earlier Senior PMM** ([job 6027839](https://careers.oneclicklca.com/jobs/6027839-senior-product-marketing-manager-for-a-sustainability-saas); [Remotive](https://remotive.com/remote/jobs/marketing/senior-product-marketing-manager-6072150)): both pages now return 410/404. The text appears to match the current ad. [Unverified]

**Conclusion:** the PMM function is young and small and is being professionalised. It moved from hiring domain experts in 2024 to hiring PMM professionals who are measured on win rate and launch adoption in 2026. It reports into the Product org.

### 2.1 CEO Panu Pasanen (identity verified: CEO & Founder)

  - "it's not based on trust. It's based on verification and control mechanism guardrails."
  - AI generates missing component datasets for MEP and building products, which "cut out potential weeks of delays and hassle".
  - Within about 18 months, "AI is proposing an entire solution, and then the user is more steering it".
  - "decarbonization is not something people often pay for separately, so it's quite important that we can make that as seamless as possible." This frames carbon as part of workflow efficiency, not a separate purchase.
  - On SimaPro: "One Click LCA is very much... design and business process driven software... SimaPro... has a science background... the ability to go deep with SimaPro, and... the ability to go fast on One Click LCA side."   - Team: "people in maybe 30, 35 countries... over 300".

**Cross-segment patterns**
1. The **compliance urgency** message lands, but the **"win business"** and growth framing is what the CEO and recent ads push. Manufacturers buy to keep market access and win specifications. AEC firms buy to win projects and credits.
2. **Support is the moat, and UX is the weak point.** Reviews praise named people and criticise the interface, the BIM round-trip and search. This matters for the "1 million users" and self-serve story.
3. **Tally is the reference point for the Revit UX** and **EC3 is the free benchmark** on price. The PMM needs a "why pay" answer against free alternatives and the cost of doing nothing.
4. Licence gating, where features depend on tier, causes friction that reviewers notice. Packaging is a PMM lever.
