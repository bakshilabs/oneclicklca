# EPD buyers: the manufacturer's view (Nordic mid-market)

Prepared 11 November 2026 for a campaign selling EPD software to mid-sized Nordic construction product manufacturers with no in-house LCA team. Proposed buyer: commercial director (head of sales). Proposed user: sustainability or quality manager.

Method: web search and public sources, 11 Nov 2026. Quotes are exact unless marked as a paraphrase or translation. Translations from Swedish, Danish, Norwegian and Finnish are mine. Individuals are referred to by role only.

Labels:
- **[V]** verified on the primary source (opened and read)
- **[S]** read on a secondary source, or seen only in a search snippet
- **[VC]** vendor claim. Many demand surveys are run by EPD software vendors, One Click LCA included, so they have a commercial interest
- **UNVERIFIED** could not confirm; check before using in copy

---

## Headline findings

1. **Demand is real but mostly arrives second-hand.** In the most rigorous manufacturer survey found (DGNB/BPIE, Germany, n=59), over 85% make EPDs because of customer demand, but 55% say the requests come from certification auditors or consultants, not from the buyer directly. In the Nordics, the drivers are regulation (Danish BR18 LCA limits, Swedish climate declarations, Norwegian public procurement climate weighting, the Finnish Building Act) and contractors' supplier requirements.
2. **A consultant-made EPD costs about EUR 5,000 to 20,000 and takes months.** Danish industry bodies quote DKK 100,000 to 150,000 per EPD and "several months". The German survey puts the typical cost at EUR 8,000 to 12,000, with verifier queues of 3 to 4 months.
3. **The "we only need a few" objection is backed by evidence.** Interviewees in the DGNB/BPIE study said a tool pays off "from eight to ten EPDs per year". Below that, a consultant can look cheaper. Mid-sized firms with a handful of products sit right on that line.
4. **Nordic programmes are growing fast.** ECO Platform status at 1 July 2026: EPD-Global (Norway) 10,718, EPD Danmark 658, RTS EPD (Finland) 505. The International EPD System has passed 20,000 EPDs, 10.3% of them from Sweden. EPD-Norge had 1,700 EPDs in 2021 and 3,259 at the start of 2024. EPD Danmark had 39 in 2019 and 408 in October 2023.
5. **No public source names who in a manufacturer decides or pays.** Manufacturer statements and guidance point to technical, quality and sustainability roles plus production data owners. Sales is the reason for buying, not the stated owner. See section 4.
6. **Evidence of sales gains is thin and qualitative.** The best figure: 27% of German manufacturers say products with EPDs are in greater demand, 44% see no change, and the study's authors warn that this does not prove a causal link. No Nordic source with quantified sales gains was found.

## 2. Why manufacturers commission EPDs, and what stops them

### Motives

- **Germany (DGNB/BPIE 2025, n=59) [V]:** "For over 85% of respondents, the motivation to provide EPDs stems from customer demand. More than two thirds cite their own climate targets or corporate strategies, 62% cite environmental communication and 60% are preparing for future regulation."
- **Global manufacturers (One Click LCA 2025) [V] [VC]:** nearly 50% see improved market position as the main benefit, and nearly 40% say EPDs are necessary to stay competitive. In 2026, "56% of manufacturers now see product-level EPDs as a competitive advantage", "a marked change from the 40% in the 2025 report."
- **Norway (Hydro, head of sustainability, 2020) [V]:** "Vi ser en økende etterspørsel fra kunder etter dokumentasjon på hvor store utslipp det knyttes til produktene våre." (We see growing demand from customers for documentation of the emissions linked to our products.) NHO, 26 Jun 2020 (URL above).
- **Finland:** "Asiakkaiden odotukset ohjaavat vahvasti Finnfoamin EPD-strategiaa." (Customer expectations strongly guide Finnfoam's EPD strategy.) 18 Nov 2025 [V] [VC]
- **Finland (Rudus, concrete):** partnered with One Click LCA because the low number and availability of EPDs had slowed the low-carbon transition of concrete in Finland. [S] [VC] (press release in search results, not opened)

## 4. Who in a manufacturer decides and pays for EPDs

No survey, programme operator or association source found states which role holds the EPD budget. What the evidence shows:

- **The legal owner is the company.** Programme fees are invoiced "to the EPD owner" and scaled by company turnover (EPD-Norge fee schedule) [V]. EPD Hub EPDs state that the manufacturer has sole ownership, liability and responsibility [S].
- **The roles that appear in public statements are technical and sustainability roles:** Technical Development Director (Finnfoam, FI) [V]; head of sustainability (Hydro, NO, 2020) [V]; association manager (Aluminium Danmark) [V]. One Click LCA's own testimonials quote an LCA services manager, a sustainability consultant, an "Applied Sustainability Expert" and a "Sustainability Coordinator" [V] [VC]. A UK case study quotes an administrative assistant who ran the EPD work [V] [VC]. **No published statement from a sales or commercial director was found.**
- **Byggmaterialindustrierna's process guide** lists production, purchasing, environment and logistics as the people to involve, and does not mention sales. Its "Steg 1" frames the start as a management choice: "Ta beslut om väg framåt ... Ett företag kan välja att arbeta med ett digitalt EPD-verktyg, anlita en konsult, eller kombinera båda" (Decide the way forward. A company can choose a digital EPD tool, hire a consultant, or both) and advises getting quotes from several providers. Aug 2025, p. 11 [V]
- **The motive is commercial even when the owner is not.** 85%+ make EPDs because customers ask (DGNB) [V]. About 50% name market position as the main benefit (One Click LCA) [V] [VC]. 75% use the results to communicate product benefits (DGNB) [V]. Requests arrive mainly through certification consultants and contractors, which is to say through the sales channel.
- **Buying process:** "manufacturers typically request several offers for the service, with reputation and experience being important criteria for many" (DGNB/BPIE) [V]. In other words, EPDs are bought as a project-based service, usually one at a time.

**Inference (not sourced):** in a mid-sized firm (roughly 50 to 500 staff) with no LCA team, the request usually starts in sales, when a tender, contractor or consultant asks for an EPD. The work then lands on the quality, technical or environment manager. Spend at EUR 10,000 to 20,000 or more probably needs sign-off from the managing director or CFO. Commercial directors who own the budget: UNVERIFIED.

**Software reviews:** Capterra and G2 pages returned 403 on 11 Nov 2026, so reviewer job titles could not be checked. From earlier research (23 Oct 2026): One Click LCA on Capterra 4.6/5 from 18 reviews, with value for money the lowest sub-score at 4.0 (see `research/competitors.md`). Vendor comparison tables show tiny review counts for EPD tools (Ecochain: 2 reviews) [S] [VC]. Review sites are not a usable source for EPD buyer personas.

## 6. Evidence of sales or specification gains after publishing EPDs

- **Best available data (DGNB/BPIE, Germany, 2025) [V]:** "27% stated that products with EPDs are in greater demand. 44% are experiencing the same level of demand, while only just over 3% are seeing lower demand." The authors add a caveat: because companies "often focus on best sellers or environmental products, this cannot be taken as evidence of a link between improved environmental performance and higher demand."
- **Hydro (Norway, 2020):** aims to "øke salget av produkter med redusert klimautslipp" (increase sales of products with reduced climate emissions). This is an intention, not a measured result. [V]
- **Finnfoam (Finland, 2025):** an EPD secures access to tenders, and accurate, usable data "muuttuu vahvaksi kilpailueduksi" (becomes a strong competitive advantage). Qualitative only. [V] [VC]
- **Vendor case studies (One Click LCA, others):** headlines such as "competitive edge" (Trimo) and "helping them win business" (Hydrotec), but no figures for revenue, win rate or specification. [V] [VC]
- **Quantified "won 3 projects" examples** on consultancy sites are illustrative and unnamed. UNVERIFIED; do not use.

**Gap:** no Nordic manufacturer has published a quantified sales or specification uplift attributed to EPDs. The defensible message is about access ("no EPD, no shortlist" in tenders, BR18, public procurement and contractor requirements), not uplift.

## Implications for the campaign (analysis)

- **Commercial director as primary buyer: credible as the person who feels the pain and starts the purchase, weak as the stated budget owner.** The trigger is commercial (customer and tender requests, competitive position), which suits a sales leader. But every public statement and process guide puts the work and the visible ownership in technical, quality or sustainability roles, and first-time spend of EUR 10,000 or more probably needs sign-off from the managing director. Recommended approach: target the commercial director with a message about lost or at-risk tenders and time to quote, and the managing director with total cost (tool versus consultant at 3 to 10 EPDs). Equip the quality or sustainability manager, who will usually run the evaluation and may veto on workload.
- **Expect the "we only need a few" objection and price for it.** Independent evidence puts the break-even at about 8 to 10 EPDs a year for bespoke tools. Show the cost per EPD at 3, 5 and 10 EPDs against a consultant price of EUR 8,000 to 15,000, and include updates every five years, product variants and verifier waiting time.
- **Lead with data collection, not LCA expertise.** Lack of internal data and staff time ranks above consultant cost as a barrier (DGNB). The user persona's pain is collecting plant data alongside the day job.
- **Use association channels.** Aluminium Danmark, Danmarks Farve- og Limindustri and EPD-Global's tool grant show associations subsidising tools for exactly this segment.
- **Avoid unsupported claims of a sales uplift.** Use the 27% "greater demand" figure with its caveat, or talk about access to tenders.
