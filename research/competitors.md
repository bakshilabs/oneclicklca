# One Click LCA: Competitive Landscape (as of 23 October 2026)

Prepared for: Senior Product Marketing Manager interview prep.
Method: web research on 23 Oct 2026. Every claim has a source. Labels used:
- **[V]** verified on a primary or credible secondary source (cited)
- **[VC]** vendor's own claim (marketing, not independently checked)
- **[U]** unverified, conflicting, or from a weak aggregator. Check before repeating in an interview
- **"n/f"** not found in research. Absent, not "doesn't exist"

> **Big caveat for the interview:** Several items in the brief no longer apply as written. **SimaPro/PRé is now part of One Click LCA** (Sept 2025). **Tally has left Building Transparency** (acquired by C.Scale, Apr 2026). **Bionova is One Click LCA's own legal entity**, not a competitor. **"Preconstruct", "Kaya" and "Sustainalize"** turned up no LCA vendor (see §6).

## 1. Building / project LCA competitors (design, engineering, contractors)

### 1.1 EC3 (Building Transparency), free
- **Segment:** North American architects, engineers, contractors, owners and procurement (Buy Clean). Manufacturers list EPDs.
- **Use case:** Find and compare product EPDs, material-level embodied carbon (A1-A3) for procurement and specs. Project-level carbon budgets.
- **Pricing:** Core tools free. **EC3 2.0 public beta announced 20 Oct 2026 at Greenbuild**. Core stays free, plus a new paid **membership programme** (price not published) [V, single source]. A Net Zero Compare listing showing "USD 5,000/yr" conflicts with this [U].
- **Database:** "250,000+ EPDs, 200+ categories, 70,000+ users, 2,000 manufacturers, 19,000 projects, 80+ countries" (Oct 2026, [U] single trade source). June 2025 board letter: ~200,000 EPDs, 50,000+ users, AI PDF parser with human review [V].
- **Compliance:** Buy Clean, LEED material credits (general knowledge [U]). Not a national-regulation WLCA tool.
- **BIM:** Integrations via partners (cove.tool 2023, Tally, C.Scale). Revit import in partner tools.
- **EPD generation:** No (database/aggregator). ODCi open-data initiative is pending.
- **Funding:** Nonprofit 501(c)(3). Supported by ClimateWorks, Amazon, Google. ODCi funded by Laudes Foundation and the Autodesk Foundation pilot.
- **Where it wins vs OCL:** Price (free), neutrality ("nonprofit, open"), the de facto US procurement EPD database, specifier habit in North America.
- Sources: [OneStopESG 20 Oct 2026](https://onestopesg.com/esg-news/building-transparency-ec3-ai-embodied-carbon), [BT board letter 5 Jun 2025](https://www.buildingtransparency.org/?p=25840426), [Net Zero Compare](https://netzerocompare.com/software/ec3-tool)

### 1.2 Tally / tallyLCA, status changed
- **Status:** KieranTimberlake gifted Tally to Building Transparency in 2021. Tally v1.0 updated for Revit 2025/2026 (2025). **C.Scale acquired Tally from Building Transparency, announced 7 Apr 2026** [V]. C.Scale plans to build and launch Tally 2.0 with KieranTimberlake. No date or price announced.
- **Segment:** US architects working in Revit.
- **Pricing:** Historical USD 1,200/yr per floating licence [U, old launch PR]. BT said v2.0 would lower the licence cost (Jun 2025). Current price n/f.
- **Database:** Historically GaBi/Sphera-based. v2.0 uses different datasets (BT, Jun 2025) and integrates EC3.
- **Where it wins:** Native Revit workflow, US AIA/architect brand loyalty. Now paired with C.Scale's concept-stage modeling (concept → BIM → EC3 procurement, all US-centric).
- **C.Scale:** PBC, spun out of EHDD in 2024 (founders Jack Rusk, Brad Jacobson). Free sign-up. Claims 200M+ sq ft modeled by enterprise partners in 2025 [VC]. Funding n/f.
- Sources: [BT: C.Scale acquires Tally](https://www.buildingtransparency.org/c-scale-acquires-tally/), [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/c-scale-acquisition-of-tally-expands-continuous-workflow-for-building-carbon-analysis), [BT Revit 2025/26](https://www.buildingtransparency.org/?p=25840448), [KT price PR (undated)](https://kierantimberlake.com/updates/press-release-kierantimberlake-and-pe-international-announce-the-availability-of-tally-tm-an-autodesk-r-revit-r-application-for-life-cycle-assessment-in-building-design/)

### 1.3 cove (cove.tool / Vitras.ai)
- **Segment:** US architects, early design and performance (energy, daylight, cost), now positioned as an "AI-native architecture firm" with Vitras.ai.
- **Embodied carbon:** Optional module using EC3 data (integration announced Jun 2023).
- **Pricing:** Subscription, not published. Funding: USD 30M round (older, TechCrunch). 2026 revenue projected at USD 10-15M, not yet profitable (Commercial Observer, Oct 2026) [VC via interview].
- **Where it wins:** Bundles carbon into early-design performance optimisation. Carbon is a feature, not a compliance product. Low threat on compliance, some threat on "good-enough early-stage carbon".
- Sources: [BusinessWire Jun 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230629055817/en/cove.tool-Announces-Integration-with-Building-Transparency%E2%80%99s-EC3-Tool), [Commercial Observer 2026](https://commercialobserver.com/power-series/2026-power-tech-ai/player/sandeep-ahuja/)

### 1.4 Cerclos (eTool / eToolLCD)
- **Segment:** Australia/NZ, plus UK and international. ESD consultants, infrastructure.
- **Use case:** Whole-of-life building and infrastructure LCA, 30+ indicators. Templates. Third-party review service of eTool studies.
- **Compliance:** Green Star, ISCA, LEED, BREEAM, CEEQUAL [VC/trade press].
- **Pricing:** n/f. BIM: not stated in sources.
- **Example:** METRONET Lakelands station (Perth), 120-year whole-of-life carbon.
- **Where it wins:** Australian heritage and Green Star/ISCA familiarity, consultant-led review service, infrastructure.
- Sources: [ISCA listing](https://iscouncil.org/?p=10928), [Capterra AU eTool](https://www.capterra.com.au/software/1060077/etool), [Capterra UK OCL vs eTool](https://www.capterra.co.uk/compare/166103/1060077/one-click-lca/vs/etool), [Fifth Estate](https://thefifthestate.com.au/innovation/rating-tools/etool-offers-life-cycle-assessments-from-the-bottom-to-the-top/)

### 1.5 Preoptima (UK), possibly what the brief means by "Preconstruct" [U]
- **Segment:** UK architects, developers, consultants, and local planning authorities (PACER platform for enforcing WLC policy).
- **Use case:** CONCEPT, AI-assisted early-stage whole life carbon. WholeLifeCarbon API for AEC software.
- **Compliance:** RICS WLCA 2nd ed. (per OCL's own comparison) [VC]. Not on the RICS validated list as of Oct 2026 [V].
- **Funding:** Pre-seed Mar 2023 with Nemetschek Group (plus Carbon13, Climate VC). Amount conflicts (USD 1.2M vs GBP 500k) [U].
- **BIM:** Revit/SketchUp plugins described as "upcoming" (OCL article, Oct 2025) [U].
- **Where it wins:** Concept-stage speed. Planning-authority channel (the regulator side, which OCL does not target directly). Nemetschek relationship (Archicad/Allplan ecosystem).
- Sources: [CIMdata / Nemetschek invests](https://www.cimdata.com/en/industry-summary-articles/item/20948-nemetschek-group-invests-in-startup-preoptima-to-drive-sustainability-in-the-construction-industry), [Architosh Mar 2023](https://architosh.com/2023/03/nemetschek-group-invests-in-world-first-carbon-hub-app-preoptima/), [OCL "Best LCA software of 2025", 8 Oct 2025](https://oneclicklca.com/en/resources/articles/building-lca-software)

### 1.6 Autodesk native carbon (Forma Carbon Insights, Embodied Carbon Analysis, Autodesk Takeoff carbon accounting): partner *and* substitute
- Forma Carbon Insights: total carbon (embodied A1-A3 per EN 15978, plus operational) in early design. Takeoff carbon accounting for estimators [V].
- **Where it wins:** Already in the designer's/estimator's seat at no extra procurement step. "Good enough" A1-A3 early numbers can delay buying a full LCA tool. Mitigated today because OCL is an Autodesk partner and full-scope/compliance stays with OCL.
- Sources: [Forma Carbon Insights](https://www.autodesk.com/products/forma-carbon-insights/overview), [Autodesk Takeoff carbon](https://www.autodesk.com/learn/ondemand/course/construction-estimating/unit/6DguXN9IXa36w3jjwXMGqC)

- **Where it wins:** Free, long-standing LEED path in North America, Canadian public-sector familiarity.

### 1.9 The "do-nothing" option: consultants plus Excel
- Free Excel tools (IStructE SCT), free data (ICE, BECD, CO2data, EC3) and consultant-led studies stay the default for low-frequency users.
- Consultant EPD cost benchmarks: ~USD 15,000 average (OCL-cited survey [VC]). EUR 5,000-15,000 per product (Emidat, May 2026 [VC]). ~USD 20,000 and 6-12 months (Pathways via Dealroom [VC]).
- University of Salford study: UK open-source embodied carbon tools give inconsistent results (system boundaries, assumptions). A credibility argument *against* spreadsheets [V].
- Sources: [OCL EPD cost](https://oneclicklca.com/en/resources/articles/cost-of-documenting-products-with-epds/), [Salford](https://salford-repository.worktribe.com/OutputFile/1488833)

## 3. Product LCA / EPD tools (manufacturers): where most head-to-head deals now are

### 3.1 SimaPro (PRé Sustainability): **now One Click LCA group**
- Acquired 2 Sep 2025. Product lines continue separately, with joint investment in data, AI and compliance [V].
- **Segment:** LCA experts, consultants, academia, multi-industry (chemicals, food, apparel, medical, tech).
- **Pricing (2025 list):** SimaPro Craft **Expert EUR 6,100/yr first user** (extra EUR 5,000). **Power EUR 7,800** (extra EUR 6,300). ecoinvent EN 15804 library EUR 240/yr. 2026: annual or up to 4-year prepaid licences. 2026 price list n/f [V for 2025].
- **Database:** ecoinvent, Agri-footprint and others. 250,000+ datasets, ~40 impact methods (per OCL) [VC].
- **EPD:** Calculates but does not publish EPDs (per OCL) [VC].
- **Interview angle:** No longer a competitor. It is the "expert" tier of the portfolio. Risk: channel conflict, positioning overlap with the OCL EPD Generator, and SimaPro partner/reseller ecosystem nerves.
- Sources: [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/one-click-lca-acquires-simapro-and-pre-sustainability-to-offer-carbon-footprint-solutions-across-industries/), [SimaPro order form 2025.3](https://simapro.se/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/2025.3-Order-form-SimaPro-plans-for-business-subscription.pdf), [ESU Services](https://esu-services.ch/simapro/professional/), [simapro.se licences](https://simapro.se/en/products-licenses)

### 3.2 Sphera LCA for Experts (formerly GaBi) / LCA Automation

- **Database:** Managed LCA Content (MLC), 20,000+ DEKRA-verified datasets [VC].
- **EPD:** Works with International EPD System, Smart EPD, IBU, UL, PEP ecopassport, INIES, EPDItaly [VC]. Verification often via Sphera consulting.

- **Pricing:** Not published.
- **Where it wins vs OCL:** Enterprise EHS/ESG suite cross-sell, deep process modeling, chemicals/auto heritage, proprietary database, global consulting arm. Strongest rival for large multi-plant building-materials groups.
- Sources: [Sphera GaBi legacy](https://sphera.com/lca-for-experts-the-gabi-legacy-advanced-by-sphera/), [Saint-Gobain case](https://sphera.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Saint-Gobain-Customer.pdf), [Energy Manager Feb 2026](https://www.energymanagermagazine.co.uk/sphera-tackles-scope-3-emissions-reporting-with-launch-of-its-automated-life-cycle-assessment-solution/), [Sphera EPD services](https://spherasolutions.com/solutions/sustainability-consulting/product-sustainability-consulting/environmental-product-declaration-epd-services/), [Verdantix on Blackstone](https://www.verdantix.com/client-portal/blog/blackstone-acquires-sphera-for-1-4-billion-signalling-a-strategic-push-into-esg-digital-solutions)

## 7. Competitive summary by segment

| Segment | Most important alternative | Why | OCL's best counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| US architects/designers | **EC3 + C.Scale/Tally** (free/low-cost, US-native, Revit) | Price and neutrality. Tally now in a funded concept-to-BIM workflow | Compliance breadth, Autodesk partnership, global EPD data, Carbon Designer 3D |
| UK consultants/contractors | **CarboniCa** (RICS-validated) + Preoptima + Excel/BECD | OCL's "first and only RICS-validated" message is now "first of two" | RICS v2 + GLA bundle, data QC, multi-country |
| Nordics / FR / NL single-country users | **National tools** (LCAbyg paid but cheaper, BM Free, ELODIE, NMD tools) | Official endorsement, local language, low price | Multi-scheme bundles, EPBD 2028 readiness, cross-border portfolios |
| Building-product manufacturers SME/mid | **Ecochain**, **Emidat**, EPD Hub packs, association tools (GCCA, Climate Earth) | Lower entry price, AI narrative, sector credibility | Pre-verified generator across 8 operators, specifier reach (Manufacturer Pages), 500k datasets |
| Enterprise multi-plant manufacturers | **Sphera** (incumbent) and **Makersite** (EUR 60M, PLM-native) | Enterprise suite cross-sell. PLM/BOM scale | SimaPro + OCL portfolio, construction-specific depth |
| LCA experts/academia | **openLCA** (free) | Free | SimaPro (now in-house) |
| Low-frequency users | **Consultant + spreadsheet** | No subscription for 1-2 projects/yr | Per-project/consultant licences, cost-per-EPD math vs EUR 5-20k consultant EPDs |

**OCL exposure flags to raise thoughtfully in the interview:** (1) value-for-money is the lowest Capterra sub-score. (2) Free/cheap national tools plus free data (EC3, BECD, CO2data) erode the low end. (3) The RICS exclusivity claim has expired. (4) AI-native EPD startups (Emidat, Pathways) and Makersite's war chest are competing on "speed + AI", which is OCL's own message. (5) SimaPro integration creates positioning overlap and reseller and channel risk. (6) Programme operators pre-verifying many tools commoditise "fast verified EPDs".
