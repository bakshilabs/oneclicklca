## 7. Gaps and cautions

1. No revenue, win-rate or tender-count figures in any case study. Commercial proof is qualitative.
2. No published One Click LCA price or cost per EPD. Only EPD Hub's €2,850 single-EPD fee and the Glob-el €12,000 consultant benchmark are public.
3. "10x faster" has no published baseline or methodology.
4. The Pre-Verified EPD Generator page redirects to the standard page in the UK, US, Finland and Sweden. Confirm the product is still sold there before using "Pre-Verified" in those markets.
5. METSEC, Uponor, Abetong, Lindab, Wienerberger and Peikko are logos only, with no public outcomes.
6. The two headline scale stories (Blue Marble/Q-railing, Thorelund/Centrum Pile) are consultant-led, which weakens a "no in-house LCA team" message. Glob-el, Vindunor, Saudi Readymix and Kiilto are the in-house stories.
7. EPD Usage Analytics: no customer case study shows what a manufacturer learned or won from the data.
8. Current pre-verification approvals per programme operator (RTS, environdec, EPD Australasia, INIES, UL) are not confirmed on operator sites.
