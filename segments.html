    In practice

    How I would use it before a launch

    Agree the weights first

    Product, sales and product marketing set the weights before any scores, so the debate stays on what the launch is for.

    Then agree the scores

    Each tier one or tier two launch scores the sub-segments with the latest pipeline and win-rate data.

    Top two or three get the launch

    They get the campaign, the enablement and an adoption target. The other segments get the release note.
